About us

70 years in the Grand Valley

The Robison family has been working on Grand Valley homes and businesses for 70 years. Four generations later, we still run the company the way it started: show up when we say we will, do the job right and stand behind it.

Most people here know us as Robison Roof Service, the family roofing company that has covered homes, shops and commercial buildings across Western Colorado. Today the same family also builds and remodels as Robison Builders, taking on kitchens, baths, additions, decks and new homes with the same crew mentality and the same attention to detail.

When you call us, you talk to a Robison. We give honest recommendations, written itemized estimates and clear updates from start to finish, and we treat every job site like it's our own home.
