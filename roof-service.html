Roof coatings

Add years to a commercial roof without tearing it off

A Lucas spray-applied coating seals an aging metal or flat roof in one seamless layer, reflects summer heat and costs a fraction of a replacement.

  • Seamless, waterproof membrane
  • Reflective white finish
  • No tear-off or landfill waste
  • Minimal disruption to your business

How we coat a roof

  1. Power wash the entire roof surface.
  2. Seal every fastener, seam and penetration, and embed RH-70 reinforcing fabric with Lucas 6500 Repair Shield.
  3. Spray the first full coat of Lucas 6000 coating.
  4. Spray the second full coat of Lucas 6000 for a complete, even film.

Coatings are priced by the roofing square. 1 square = 100 sq ft of roof.

Storm damage & insurance claims

Hail or wind damage? We'll help you through the claim

Western Colorado storms are hard on roofs. We inspect for damage, document it and work alongside your insurance adjuster, so you know exactly what your roof needs.

  1. Free storm damage inspection

    We check shingles, metal, flashing, vents and gutters for hail and wind damage.

  2. Photo documentation

    You get clear photos and measurements of the damage to share with your insurance company.

  3. Meet your adjuster

    We can meet the adjuster on site to walk the roof and point out the damage. You stay in charge of your claim.

  4. Repair or replace

    Once your claim is settled, we repair or replace the roof and handle cleanup.
